  Forever keeps Engineering’s skill ranges and adds 80 recipes, five of them using new materials.

  utility nobody else has.

steps:
  - id: prof-engineering-001
    from: 1
    to: 50
    action: >-
      Learn Engineering and make copper bolts, endlessly. You will want Mining or deep pockets:
      nothing eats bars faster.
    materials:
      - item: Copper Bar
        quantity: 80
  - id: prof-engineering-002
    from: 50
    to: 105
    action: >-
      Tubes, explosive charges and the first bronze pieces.
    materials:
      - item: Copper Bar
        quantity: 60
      - item: Bronze Bar
        quantity: 60
      - item: Weak Flux
        quantity: 20
  - id: prof-engineering-003
    from: 105
    to: 150
    action: >-
      Bronze and iron, with powder along the way. First Legacy point at 150.
    materials:
      - item: Bronze Bar
        quantity: 60
      - item: Iron Bar
        quantity: 40
      - item: Coarse Blasting Powder
        quantity: 60
    note: >-
      The Legacy point is paid once per account, not per character.
  - id: prof-engineering-004
    from: 150
    to: 200
    action: >-
      Iron and steel: the grenade band, and the first devices actually worth carrying.
    materials:
      - item: Iron Bar
        quantity: 100
      - item: Heavy Blasting Powder
        quantity: 60
  - id: prof-engineering-005
    from: 200
    to: 225
    action: >-
      Mithril to 225 and the second Legacy point. This is where you pick a specialisation, gnomish or
      goblin.
    materials:
      - item: Mithril Bar
        quantity: 80
    note: >-
      The Legacy point is paid once per account, not per character.
  - id: prof-engineering-006
    from: 225
    to: 260
    action: >-
      Mithril and the first thorium pieces. Keep any gems you turn up: several recipes ask for them.
    materials:
      - item: Mithril Bar
        quantity: 100
      - item: Solid Blasting Powder
        quantity: 60
  - id: prof-engineering-007
    from: 260
    to: 300
    action: >-
      Thorium to 300. Before you start, check which recipes use the new materials: five of them did
      not exist in Classic.
    materials:
      - item: Thorium Bar
        quantity: 150
      - item: Dense Blasting Powder
        quantity: 80
  - id: prof-engineering-008
    from: 300
    to: 300
    action: >-
      Take the Engineering certification. From here the profession pays for itself in utility nobody
      else brings.
