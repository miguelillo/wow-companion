  You cannot gather it and you cannot easily buy it: it runs on disenchanting, which is why it
  pairs with Tailoring or Leatherworking to give you something to destroy. Expensive to level, and
  the best earner afterwards, because enchants are consumed and asked for again.

steps:
  - id: prof-enchanting-001
    from: 1
    to: 60
    action: >-
      Learn Enchanting and disenchant every spare green and white. Enchant bracers with lesser
      essences.
    materials:
      - item: Strange Dust
        quantity: 60
      - item: Lesser Magic Essence
        quantity: 20
  - id: prof-enchanting-002
    from: 60
    to: 110
    action: >-
      Bracer and chest enchants on dust and lesser essences. With Tailoring you can craft and
      disenchant in a loop.
    materials:
      - item: Strange Dust
        quantity: 80
      - item: Greater Magic Essence
        quantity: 30
  - id: prof-enchanting-003
    from: 110
    to: 150
    action: >-
      Soul dust and astral essences. First Legacy point at 150.
    materials:
      - item: Soul Dust
        quantity: 60
      - item: Lesser Astral Essence
        quantity: 30
    note: >-
      The Legacy point is paid once per account, not per character.
  - id: prof-enchanting-004
    from: 150
    to: 200
    action: >-
      Vision dust and mystic essences. The enchants people pay for start here.
    materials:
      - item: Vision Dust
        quantity: 80
      - item: Greater Mystic Essence
        quantity: 40
  - id: prof-enchanting-005
    from: 200
    to: 225
    action: >-
      Dream dust to 225 and the second Legacy point.
    materials:
      - item: Dream Dust
        quantity: 60
    note: >-
      The Legacy point is paid once per account, not per character.
  - id: prof-enchanting-006
    from: 225
    to: 265
    action: >-
      Dream dust and nether essences. The most expensive band in the profession: arrive with material
      to spare.
    materials:
      - item: Dream Dust
        quantity: 100
      - item: Greater Nether Essence
        quantity: 40
  - id: prof-enchanting-007
    from: 265
    to: 300
    action: >-
      Illusion dust and eternal essences to 300, with shards for the good ones.
    materials:
      - item: Illusion Dust
        quantity: 120
      - item: Large Brilliant Shard
        quantity: 20
  - id: prof-enchanting-008
    from: 300
    to: 300
    action: >-
      Take the Enchanting certification and set up shop: at 300 you sell a service, not an item.
