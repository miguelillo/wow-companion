  Forever leaves Alchemy’s skill ranges alone, so the ladder is the familiar one, but it adds 72

  earner rather than just the place you make your own potions.

steps:
  - id: prof-alchemy-001
    from: 1
    to: 60
    action: >-
      Learn Alchemy and make Minor Healing Potions to 60. It is the cheapest band in the whole
      profession.
    materials:
      - item: Peacebloom
        quantity: 30
      - item: Silverleaf
        quantity: 30
      - item: Crystal Vial
        quantity: 30
  - id: prof-alchemy-002
    from: 60
    to: 110
    action: >-
      Push on with Lesser Healing Potions. Stockpile Briarthorn before you start: it is the
      bottleneck.
    materials:
      - item: Briarthorn
        quantity: 40
      - item: Crystal Vial
        quantity: 40
  - id: prof-alchemy-003
    from: 110
    to: 150
    action: >-
      Lesser elixirs and Healing Potions to 150. Reaching it pays your first Legacy point.
    materials:
      - item: Bruiseweed
        quantity: 40
      - item: Mageroyal
        quantity: 30
    note: >-
      The Legacy point is paid once per account, not per character.
  - id: prof-alchemy-004
    from: 150
    to: 190
    action: >-
      The Kingsblood and Liferoot band. This is where elixirs people actually buy start appearing.
    materials:
      - item: Kingsblood
        quantity: 40
      - item: Liferoot
        quantity: 30
      - item: Leaded Vial
        quantity: 50
  - id: prof-alchemy-005
    from: 190
    to: 225
    action: >-
      Skill up on whatever uses Khadgar’s Whisker and Goldthorn. Second Legacy point at 225.
    materials:
      - item: Khadgar’s Whisker
        quantity: 40
      - item: Goldthorn
        quantity: 30
    note: >-
      The Legacy point is paid once per account, not per character.
  - id: prof-alchemy-006
    from: 225
    to: 265
    action: >-
      Sungrass and Blindweed. From here each point costs more herbs: buy in bulk if your time is worth
      more than the gold.
    materials:
      - item: Sungrass
        quantity: 50
      - item: Crystal Vial
        quantity: 60
  - id: prof-alchemy-007
    from: 265
    to: 300
    action: >-
      Dreamfoil and Mountain Silversage to 300. This is the expensive band: do not start it without
      the herbs already banked.
    materials:
      - item: Dreamfoil
        quantity: 60
      - item: Mountain Silversage
        quantity: 40
  - id: prof-alchemy-008
    from: 300
    to: 300
    action: >-
      At 300 take the Alchemy certification: it unlocks a permanent account-wide title. Then aim at
      the new flasks and reputation elixirs, which is where the gold is.
    note: >-
      The 300 certification exists for Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking
      and Tailoring.
