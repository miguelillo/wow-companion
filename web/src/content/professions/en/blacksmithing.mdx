steps:
  - id: prof-blacksmithing-001
    from: 1
    to: 65
    action: >-
      Learn Blacksmithing and hammer out copper studs and bracers. You need Mining or a steady
      supplier: no craft eats more material.
    materials:
      - item: Copper Bar
        quantity: 90
  - id: prof-blacksmithing-002
    from: 65
    to: 105
    action: >-
      Bronze chains and bracers. Smelt tin and copper into bronze before you start.
    materials:
      - item: Bronze Bar
        quantity: 100
  - id: prof-blacksmithing-003
    from: 105
    to: 150
    action: >-
      Heavy bronze and the first iron pieces. Watch the panel: many recipes now skill up lower than in
      Classic, so do not over-craft.
    materials:
      - item: Bronze Bar
        quantity: 80
      - item: Iron Bar
        quantity: 40
    note: >-
      First Legacy point at 150. The Legacy point is paid once per account, not per character.
  - id: prof-blacksmithing-004
    from: 150
    to: 200
    action: >-
      Iron and steel. The silver belt buckle appears here, the first of four tiers.
    materials:
      - item: Iron Bar
        quantity: 100
      - item: Steel Bar
        quantity: 60
  - id: prof-blacksmithing-005
    from: 200
    to: 225
    action: >-
      Mithril pieces to 225 and the second Legacy point.
    materials:
      - item: Mithril Bar
        quantity: 80
    note: >-
      The Legacy point is paid once per account, not per character.
  - id: prof-blacksmithing-006
    from: 225
    to: 260
    action: >-
      Mithril in bulk, and the iron buckle. This band burns the most bars.
    materials:
      - item: Mithril Bar
        quantity: 120
  - id: prof-blacksmithing-007
    from: 260
    to: 300
    action: >-
      Thorium to 300, with the mithril and arcanite buckles as the goal.
    materials:
      - item: Thorium Bar
        quantity: 150
      - item: Arcanite Bar
        quantity: 6
  - id: prof-blacksmithing-008
    from: 300
    to: 300
    action: >-
      Take the Blacksmithing certification and keep the two recipes that earn you a spot in any group:
      repair all, and the buckles.
