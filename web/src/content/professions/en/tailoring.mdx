steps:
  - id: prof-tailoring-001
    from: 1
    to: 70
    action: >-
      Learn Tailoring and sew linen bolts and bags. No gathering profession needed: cloth comes from
      killing humanoids, and now you get more of it.
    materials:
      - item: Linen Cloth
        quantity: 90
  - id: prof-tailoring-002
    from: 70
    to: 110
    action: >-
      Fine linen and the first wool pieces.
    materials:
      - item: Linen Cloth
        quantity: 60
      - item: Wool Cloth
        quantity: 60
  - id: prof-tailoring-003
    from: 110
    to: 150
    action: >-
      Wool, then silk at the end of the band. First Legacy point at 150. With the earlier skill-ups
      you will arrive sooner than you expect, so check the panel before buying more cloth.
    materials:
      - item: Wool Cloth
        quantity: 80
      - item: Silk Cloth
        quantity: 40
    note: >-
      The Legacy point is paid once per account, not per character.
  - id: prof-tailoring-004
    from: 150
    to: 200
    action: >-
      Silk in quantity: bags, bandages and gear.
    materials:
      - item: Silk Cloth
        quantity: 120
  - id: prof-tailoring-005
    from: 200
    to: 225
    action: >-
      Mageweave to 225 and the second Legacy point.
    materials:
      - item: Mageweave Cloth
        quantity: 100
    note: >-
      The Legacy point is paid once per account, not per character.
  - id: prof-tailoring-006
    from: 225
    to: 260
    action: >-
      Mageweave and the step up to runecloth. This is where a partner with Enchanting pays off,
      disenchanting what you make.
    materials:
      - item: Mageweave Cloth
        quantity: 80
      - item: Runecloth
        quantity: 60
  - id: prof-tailoring-007
    from: 260
    to: 300
    action: >-
      Runecloth to 300. Everything spare gets disenchanted or sold: do not vendor it.
    materials:
      - item: Runecloth
        quantity: 180
  - id: prof-tailoring-008
    from: 300
    to: 300
    action: >-
      Take the Tailoring certification and get on the cloak embroideries: five tiers of spell power,
      attack power or stamina, and everyone wants one.
