  your skill rather than grinding one spot for hours.

steps:
  - id: prof-mining-001
    from: 1
    to: 65
    action: >-
      Learn Mining and work copper in your starting zone and the 10–20 zones: Elwynn, Dun Morogh,
      Durotar, Tirisfal.
    materials:
      - item: Copper Ore
        quantity: 60
    note: >-
      Smelt copper into bars as you go: it stacks smaller and smelting also skills you up early on.
  - id: prof-mining-002
    from: 65
    to: 125
    action: >-
      Move to tin and silver in the 20–30 zones: Barrens, Hillsbrad, Loch Modan, Duskwood.
    materials:
      - item: Tin Ore
        quantity: 80
      - item: Silver Ore
        quantity: 10
  - id: prof-mining-003
    from: 125
    to: 150
    action: >-
      Iron in Stranglethorn, Arathi and Desolace. The first milestone lands here.
    materials:
      - item: Iron Ore
        quantity: 60
  - id: prof-mining-004
    from: 150
    to: 175
    action: >-
      Stay on iron and take any gold veins you pass.
    materials:
      - item: Iron Ore
        quantity: 60
      - item: Gold Ore
        quantity: 10
  - id: prof-mining-005
    from: 175
    to: 230
    action: >-
      Mithril in Tanaris, Feralas, the Hinterlands and Badlands. It is the ore that sells best while
      everyone is levelling crafts.
    materials:
      - item: Mithril Ore
        quantity: 100
  - id: prof-mining-006
    from: 225
    to: 250
    action: >-
      Collect your Legacy point at 225 and carry on with mithril and truesilver.
    materials:
      - item: Truesilver Ore
        quantity: 20
    note: >-
      The Legacy point for skill 225 is paid once per account, not per character.
  - id: prof-mining-007
    from: 250
    to: 300
    action: >-
      Thorium in Un’Goro, Winterspring, Burning Steppes and Silithus to 300.
    materials:
      - item: Thorium Ore
        quantity: 150
  - id: prof-mining-008
    from: 300
    to: 300
    action: >-
      At 300, decide whether to stay pure gathering or pair it with Blacksmithing or Engineering and
      craft from your own ore.
